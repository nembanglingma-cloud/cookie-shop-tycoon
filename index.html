How to Play Your first batch starts here
  1. Plan your batch. Start with $100. Choose a cookie variety and how many cookies to bake.
  2. Set your strategy. Pick a selling price and advertising budget. Your total cost, including $5 daily overhead, must fit your cash.
  3. Open your doors. Click Open shop & bake to watch customers arrive. Use See results to skip the animation.
  4. Learn and grow. Review sales, expenses, profit, and cash. Adjust tomorrow’s plan and finish 30 days with as much cash as you can!

Lower prices attract more customers; advertising boosts demand. Unsold cookies are donated each night. The shop closes early if you have less than $5. Progress saves in this browser when available.
